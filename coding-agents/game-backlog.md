# Ingredients for twenty different games

`fanout.py --init` independently shuffles these three lists without replacement
and commits twenty combined briefs to the game's `BACKLOG.md`. Each agent gets
one brief. Another participant gets different combinations; an existing backlog
is kept. The agents invent their own names, scenes, layouts and implementation.

## Mechanics

- Aim ricochet shots around obstacles to hit a sequence of targets.
- Time orbital slingshots between moving anchors without drifting away.
- Steer a drifting racer through gates, balancing speed against control.
- Swing on a grappling line and release to land on small platforms.
- Drop and balance moving pieces into a precarious tower.
- Hit different input lanes on a visible rhythm to build a combo.
- Rotate mirrors to guide a beam through changing target arrangements.
- Rotate pipe tiles to connect a source to its destination.
- Remember a briefly revealed pattern and reproduce it under pressure.
- Trace closed loops to claim territory while avoiding moving intruders.
- Place a limited number of seeds to trigger growing chain reactions.
- Switch magnetic polarity to steer around hazards toward checkpoints.
- Queue a short sequence of moves, then send a rover through a small grid.
- Dash between cover while sweeping spotlights search for you.
- Weave through patterned projectiles and collect risky close-range bonuses.
- Time a release inside a moving sweet spot, adjusting for changing tension.
- Slide rows or columns of tiles to assemble a traversable route.
- Pilot a glider between rising air currents and narrowing passages.
- Place and reposition a few defenders to intercept waves on a small board.
- Slice moving targets with pointer strokes while avoiding marked decoys.

## Art directions

- Warm risograph poster: coral, indigo and cream, grain-like dots, offset shapes.
- Cut-paper diorama: layered silhouettes, soft shadows, spacious pale background.
- Chunky pixel arcade: limited mint and plum palette, crisp shapes, bold pixel framing.
- Art deco cabinet: ivory and deep teal, brass linework, symmetrical geometric borders.
- Ink-brush scroll: expressive black strokes, vermilion accents, generous negative space.
- Retro space manual: cobalt and orange, technical diagrams, compact side instruments.
- Stained glass: jewel-tone cells, dark leading, a luminous central composition.
- Ceramic tabletop: cobalt patterns on warm white, rounded tiles and tactile shadows.
- Screen-printed comic: halftone dots, yellow and black, dramatic angled panels.
- Quiet botanical plate: sage and parchment, fine outlines, small specimen labels.
- Vaporwave postcard: lavender sunset, hot coral accents, layered horizon silhouettes.
- Blueprint machine: chalk-white lines on navy, measurement marks and side gauges.
- Folk-art tapestry: terracotta and turquoise, repeating border motifs, flat ornaments.
- Monochrome silent cinema: sepia, strong silhouettes, title cards and a filmstrip edge.
- Clay toy theater: rounded pastel forms, soft depth, a bright stage and chunky controls.
- Swiss graphic poster: red, white and charcoal, oversized type and an asymmetric grid.
- Night-vision field guide: acid green on black, restrained glow, circular instrument framing.
- Watercolor travel journal: muted blue and ochre, overlapping washes, small sketched details.
- Mid-century board game: mustard and petrol blue, geometric tokens, an off-center play area.
- Festival woodcut: aubergine and amber, carved-looking strokes, a tall theatrical frame.
